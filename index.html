Brickwall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026
Publisher: Orgbricks · orgbricks.com · hello@orgbricks.com

This policy covers the Brickwall browser extension (formerly KnotSafe).

Summary

Brickwall does not send your data anywhere. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server, and it makes no network requests of its own.

What Brickwall reads

To do its job on the pages you visit, Brickwall reads, inside your browser:

  • the hostname of the page, to tell which Salesforce environment it is and whether it should be locked;
  • the page's controls (such as buttons, links and form fields) and your clicks and edits on them, to block actions while a lock is on;
  • on Salesforce pages, the tab title and icon, to add an environment marker.

Page content, record data and what you type are not saved or transmitted.

What is stored on your device

Brickwall uses Chrome's local storage for:

  • lock settings per hostname, the all-sites setting and unlock timers;
  • environment colours and lock defaults, and org names and colours you enter;
  • always-lock rules you enter;
  • a lock history: time, hostname, lock or unlock, duration and which control was used — up to 200 entries;
  • preferences: theme, auto re-lock time, tab marker, sound, volume, auto-hide and the position of the website indicator;
  • if you set a password, a SHA-256 hash of it. The password itself is not stored.

Your control

  • Clear the lock history from the Options page.
  • Remove the password, rules and org names at any time.
  • Removing the extension deletes its local data.

Export and import

You can export settings to a file on your computer. The file includes hostnames, org names and rules, but not your password or history. Brickwall does not upload it; where you keep or share it is your choice.

Organisation-managed browsers

An administrator can push always-lock rules, org names and the re-lock time through Chrome policy. Brickwall reads these settings. It sends nothing back to the administrator.

Sharing

Brickwall does not sell or share data. It contains no trackers or remote code.

Limits

The lock changes only what you can do in your own browser. It protects against accidents; it is not a security control and does not replace Salesforce permissions. A website could observe typing in a password box shown on its page, so do not reuse an important password.

Children

Brickwall is a work tool and is not directed at children.
